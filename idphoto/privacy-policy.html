ID Photo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

ID Photo respects your privacy.

Photo Processing

Photos selected or captured by the user are processed locally on the user's device. The developer does not upload, collect, store, or share these photos.

Local Storage and Sharing

The app creates temporary photo files in its local cache during photo processing. These files remain on the user's device and are not accessible to the developer. Users can remove cached files through Android's app storage settings.

Processed results are exported to shared device storage or shared with another application only when the user chooses those actions. Users manage and delete exported files through their gallery or file manager. The destination and subsequent handling of shared photos are controlled by the application or service selected by the user.

Account Information

ID Photo does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Optional Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase to support the developer. Payments are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store complete payment card information. This purchase does not unlock or restrict any app functionality.

Bug Reports

Users may voluntarily send feedback or bug reports through an email application. The app prepares an email containing the report type, selected country and document type, app name and application ID, app version, device manufacturer and model, Android version, and the report text entered by the user. Information is transmitted only after the user reviews and sends the email.

The developer receives the sender's email address and the contents of the submitted email. These emails and their information are used solely to improve the app, fix bugs, and track related issues. They are retained for as long as necessary for these purposes. Users may request deletion of their submitted emails and associated information by contacting chickeneren43@gmail.com. The email service providers involved also process the email according to their own privacy policies.

Data Collection

The developer receives information voluntarily submitted by email as described in the Bug Reports section. Google ML Kit separately processes SDK metrics as described below.

Google ML Kit

ID Photo uses Google ML Kit for on-device photo processing. ML Kit processes input photos locally and does not send those photos or the processing results to Google servers.

ML Kit sends SDK performance, usage, and diagnostic data to Google. According to Google, this includes device and application information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature versions, event types, and error codes. Google uses these metrics to measure performance, debug, maintain and improve its APIs, and detect misuse or abuse. This also applies to features whose models are bundled with the app. The metrics are encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

For details, see Google ML Kit Terms & Privacy, ML Kit Android data disclosure, and Google Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

ID Photo is not specifically directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact chickeneren43@gmail.com.
