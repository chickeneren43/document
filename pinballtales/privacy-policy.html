Pinball Tales Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy applies to Pinball Tales (彈珠物語), Android application ID com.chickeneren43.pinballtales.

Information Collection and Sharing

The current Android version runs its game content locally. When you open the announcement window, the app requests the current announcement page from GitHub Pages over HTTPS. This request is not made during ordinary offline gameplay. GitHub receives connection information such as your IP address and request metadata to serve the page, under its own privacy statement. The app does not send saved progress or purchase records with this request, or load remote tracking scripts, images, or fonts. It does not require an account, upload gameplay data to the developer, or include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not collect, sell, or share personal information through the game.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores progress, high scores, language, sound preferences, tutorial status, and other game settings locally on your device. This data is used to resume gameplay and remember your preferences. It is not sent to the developer. Temporary testing access to paid tables is not recorded as a purchase.

Local data remains until it is reset in the game, cleared through Android app storage settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled. Clearing app storage removes saved progress and settings.

Device Features

The game uses vibration, where available and enabled, for gameplay feedback, and can adjust device audio volume in response to your controls. These features do not collect personal information. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Purchases

Google Play in-app purchases are not enabled in the current Android version. Test builds may provide temporary access to otherwise locked tables without payment. If purchases are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the processing of purchase information before that version is released.

Contacting the Developer

If you email the developer, your email provider transmits your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. The developer uses this information to respond to your request and resolve support issues, and retains it only as long as needed for those purposes. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing chickeneren43@gmail.com. Your email provider processes messages under its own privacy policy.

External Services

If you choose to open an external link, the destination website or app handles information under its own privacy policy. Google Play and Android may separately process information under Google's policies; this policy describes the game's own data practices.

Children's Privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact the email address below to request deletion.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The latest policy and its revision date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact chickeneren43@gmail.com.
